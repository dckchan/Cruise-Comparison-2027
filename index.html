Why the Sun Princess 14-Night Grand Mediterranean?
For a family of six departing from Vancouver, this itinerary offers the strongest combination of value, destination variety, and ship quality. You'll visit both Italy and Greece in a single sailing — including bucket-list ports like Santorini, Athens, Rome, and Cinque Terre — aboard Princess's newest and most technologically advanced ship. At roughly $2,158 per person for the cruise fare (interior cabin), it's significantly less expensive per night than the Legend of the Seas while still offering a modern, feature-rich ship experience. The 14-night duration means fewer embarkation hassles relative to days at sea, and Princess's complimentary dining, MedallionClass technology, and connected-room options make it exceptionally family-friendly. If budget is the primary concern, the RC Rhapsody 7-night Greek Isles from Athens is the most affordable option at approximately $19,000–$20,000 total for all six travelers. For the most luxurious ship experience and you're willing to pay a premium, the Legend of the Seas offers Royal Caribbean's newest onboard features, though at a significantly higher cruise-fare cost for a shorter voyage.

Getting There from Vancouver

None of these cruise lines depart from Vancouver for European itineraries — all Mediterranean and Northern Europe cruises embark from European ports (Barcelona, Athens, Rome, Southampton). You'll need to fly from Vancouver (YVR) to your embarkation city.

Estimated round-trip flights (summer 2027): Vancouver → Barcelona is approximately CAD $1,800–$2,200 per person (≈ USD $1,300–$1,600) via Air Canada, Lufthansa, KLM, or British Airways with one connection. Vancouver → Athens is slightly more at CAD $2,000–$2,500 (≈ USD $1,450–$1,800). Vancouver → London/Southampton averages CAD $1,500–$1,900 (≈ USD $1,100–$1,400).

Pro tip: Book flights 6–9 months ahead for summer travel. Consider arriving one day early to account for jet lag and potential delays — a pre-cruise hotel night in Barcelona or Athens is well worth the peace of mind, especially with six travelers. Look for Air Canada's direct seasonal routes to European hubs for the smoothest connections.

Getting There from Vancouver — Asia

Vancouver is well-connected to Asian hubs with direct flights on Air Canada, Cathay Pacific, and Singapore Airlines. Vancouver → Singapore is approximately CAD $1,200–$1,800 (≈ USD $900–$1,300) round trip with a direct or one-stop flight. Vancouver → Bangkok is approximately CAD $1,400–$2,000 (≈ USD $1,000–$1,450). Vancouver → Hong Kong averages CAD $1,100–$1,600 (≈ USD $800–$1,200). Flights to Asia are often cheaper than Europe in the winter season (Oct–Mar) when Asian cruises operate.

Pro tip: Asian cruises typically run from October through March (winter season), which complements the European summer season (April–September). You could potentially do a European summer cruise AND an Asian winter cruise in the same year. Vancouver's proximity to Asia means shorter flight times (10–13 hours to Singapore) compared to European destinations (9–12 hours).

Why the Quantum of the Seas 10-Night Thailand & Vietnam?
For a family of six from Vancouver, this itinerary is the standout Asia pick. At roughly $1,000 per person for the cruise fare, it costs less than half of a comparable European sailing while visiting five compelling destinations across three countries. The Quantum of the Seas offers outstanding family facilities including the SeaPlex indoor complex, iFly skydiving, FlowRider, and H2O Zone for kids. Vancouver is ideally positioned for Asia travel — Singapore is a direct or one-stop flight away — and the winter season timing (Oct–Mar) means you could pair this with a European summer cruise for the ultimate two-season year. The biggest advantage over the Celebrity and Princess alternatives is price: the Quantum delivers nearly the same ports at 40–60% less cost. If you want a more premium, intimate experience with longer port stays (including overnight stops in Bangkok and Ha Long Bay), the Celebrity Solstice 12-night is the luxury upgrade. And if your family wants the ultimate Asia mega-voyage, Princess's 31-night Singapore-to-Japan sailing on Diamond Princess is unmatched for sheer scope.

Cabin 2 holds 2 guests at standard double occupancy rates. Not all interior cabins accommodate 4 guests — you must book a cabin with pullman beds or sofa beds. Hotel costs assume 2 rooms instead of 3. 3rd/4th guest rates vary by cruise line, sailing date, and cabin category — verify exact pricing when booking. Flight costs are estimates based on current average pricing from Vancouver; actual fares will vary. Shore excursion, onboard spending, and gratuity estimates are averages and your actual costs may be higher or lower. Travel insurance is strongly recommended for international travel. Always verify current pricing directly with the cruise line or a licensed travel agent before booking. Prices do not include passport/visa fees. For Asia travel, some destinations may require advance visas depending on your nationality — check requirements early. Cruise line promotions (free drinks packages, onboard credits, kids-sail-free offers) may significantly reduce total costs — check for current offers when booking. Celebrity's current offer of "75% off 2nd Guest + 3rd, 4th, & 5th Guests Sail FREE" on select Asia sailings could dramatically reduce cruise fares. Asian cruise seasons (Oct–Mar) and European seasons (Apr–Sep) do not overlap, making it possible to do both in the same year. Cruise Critic ratings and reviews are sourced from cruisecritic.com and reflect community and editor assessments as of early 2026.
